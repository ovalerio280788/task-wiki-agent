## Context

Use RICE to compare initiatives when a prioritization decision is needed.

## What works

1. Set a shared time period for reach.
2. Estimate each input:
   - **Reach:** affected people or events in that period. For internal work, define one consistent
     opportunity unit before scoring.
   - **Impact:** expected magnitude of the outcome: 3, 2, 1, 0.5, or 0.25.
   - **Confidence:** certainty in the estimates: 100%, 80%, or 50%.
   - **Effort:** total person-time required.
3. Calculate `Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort`.
4. Present a compact table with the inputs, score, decision, and dependencies.

## Gotchas

- RICE ranks relative opportunity; it does not forecast an absolute outcome.
- Do not fabricate inputs. Mark unknowns and seek evidence before treating a ranking as decisive.
- Keep the same time period, impact scale, and effort unit across compared initiatives.
- When work has a different Reach unit, document its dependency or strategic exception instead of
  forcing it into an incomparable score.
